# Closed items

This file answers one question: was this item settled, and how?

- **An item gets one row here when it leaves [register.md](register.md); the newest row goes on top.**
- **Chosen records the choice itself.** *"Done" answers whether; the next session needs what.* A rejected option
  keeps its reason.
- **Outcome** is `done` (built, or answered as Chosen says), `superseded` (split or replaced: Chosen names the
  new IDs) or `withdrawn` (a duplicate, or no longer wanted: Chosen says which and why).
- **Reopen if** names the fact the choice rests on that could change; empty where none could.
- **A row, once written, keeps its choice.** Changing a closed decision opens a fresh register item that cites this
  one. *A rewritten row would hide when the first choice was made, and why it changed.*
- **The IDs here are taken.** The next ID in a family counts this file as well as the register.
